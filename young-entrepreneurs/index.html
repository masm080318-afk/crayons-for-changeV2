How It Works

Don't just learn business. Build one.

Every student starts with an idea and develops it week by week — choosing a customer, building a product, setting a price, and learning to explain why it matters.

01
Find an idea

Explore problems worth solving and pick one to develop.

02

03
Learn how to sell it

Practice marketing, communicating value, and closing a sale.

04
Pitch it

Prepare and deliver a pitch at the C4C Youth Business Fair.

The Program

From idea to entrepreneur.

01
Start With an Idea

Understand entrepreneurship, identify opportunities, and choose a business idea to develop.

02
Find Your Customer

Identify who the business is for and what problem it solves.

03
Build the Product

Turn the idea into a clear product or service.

04
Price It

Understand costs, revenue, profit, and how to choose a selling price.

05
Build the Brand

Create a name, visual identity, positioning, and message.

06
Market It

Learn how businesses attract attention and create a simple marketing plan.

07
Sell It

Practice communicating value, speaking with customers, and making a sale.

08
Know the Numbers

Learn basic revenue, expenses, profit, and business finance.

09
Tell the Story

Build and practice a concise business pitch.

10
Get Fair-Ready

Refine the business, rehearse, receive feedback, and prepare for the final showcase.

Real-world skills, taught by doing.

FAQ

Questions before you start?

Who is the program for?

Students in grades 4–9. Reach out if you have a question about a specific age or grade.

Does my child need any business experience?

No. The program is designed to start from the beginning. Students only need curiosity and a willingness to build.

Is the entire program online?

Yes. Weekly classes and the final Youth Business Fair are designed to take place online.

What type of business can a student create?

Students can develop an age-appropriate product or service idea. More detailed guidelines will be provided before the program begins.

Does my child need to spend money building the business?

This is still being finalized. We'll share full guidance with registered families before the program begins.

What if a student misses a class?

This is still being finalized. We'll share the full policy with registered families before the program begins.

When does the program begin?

The final schedule will be announced before registration opens.

When is the Youth Business Fair?

The virtual fair is currently planned for around December 5, 2026. Final timing will be announced to registered families.

Are scholarships available?

Yes. Need-based scholarships will be available.

Where does the registration money go?

Program proceeds support Crayons for Change's educational-access initiatives and program costs.
