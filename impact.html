What donations pay for

The goal is simple: turn money, materials, and volunteer time into tools students can use right away.

  • Supply kits: crayons, notebooks, backpacks, pencil cases, markers, folders, and classroom basics.
  • STEM materials: electronics parts, building materials, science supplies, craft materials, and printed guides.
  • Program logistics: printing, outreach, event setup, workshop materials, and fair supplies.
  • International outreach: packing, transport, and delivery costs for supplies sent or carried to students abroad.
