OUR STORY / EST. 2023

One pencil.
A whole new perspective.

A conversation with their parents inspired two young people to help put school supplies into more students’ hands.

HOW IT BEGAN

A story that
stayed with them.

In 2023, Avyukth Salver and Mohammed Mansuri were together when their parents shared memories of their own school days—including what it was like to make a single pencil last the whole year.

That conversation changed how Avyukth and Mohammed thought about the supplies they used every day. A pencil or a notebook could seem ordinary, but having one when you needed it could make a real difference.

They founded Crayons for Change that year with a simple purpose: help under-resourced students get the tools they need to learn and create. What began with listening became a youth-led effort to turn care into action.

Today, that purpose connects our school supply projects and community events. With 6,000+ school supplies distributed and work reaching 3 countries, we’re continuing the story one student, one supply, and one shared effort at a time.
