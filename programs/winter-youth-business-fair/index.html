About the program

Crayons for Change's Winter Youth Business Fair (YBF) is a 10-week online entrepreneurship program.

Throughout the program, students build their own businesses while learning:

  • Idea development
  • Pricing
  • Branding
  • Marketing
  • Finance
  • Sales
  • Pitching

Program timeline
