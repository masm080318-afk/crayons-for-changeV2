September 16, 2026Distribution date

THE SCHOOL

Greendale Residences Integrated School

Tacloban, Leyte, Philippines

Learning kits for 50 elementary and 50 junior high school students.

The kits provided additional materials for students’ everyday schoolwork, supporting Project PITAD’s focus on learning skills and literacy.

With their own supplies, students have materials to practice reading, writing, and other learning activities in school and at home.

Made possible through partnership

Crayons for Change funded the learning kits. Teach for the Philippines coordinated local distribution through its Teacher Fellows and school communities.
