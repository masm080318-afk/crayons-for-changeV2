Project LISO

Tools for everyday learning.

71Students received learning kits
September 16, 2026Distribution date

THE SCHOOL

Astorga Elementary School

Alangalang, Leyte, Philippines

Learning kits for 71 students from Kindergarten to Grade 6.

Some students needed to borrow paper before beginning classroom activities, while younger learners had already filled their notebooks. These kits gave students their own materials for daily lessons and schoolwork.

The distribution reached more than half of the school’s students, providing practical supplies they could use right away.

Made possible through partnership

Crayons for Change funded the learning kits. Teach for the Philippines coordinated local distribution through its Teacher Fellows and school communities.
