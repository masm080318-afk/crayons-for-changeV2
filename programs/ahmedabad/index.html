THE PROJECT

Ahmedabad, India

Crayons for Change’s first school-supply initiative

The Ahmedabad Project supported more than 116 students in India with 696 essential school supplies.

Students received notebooks, pencils, pens, crayons, erasers, and other classroom materials. These practical supplies helped students in underserved communities begin the school year with the basic tools they needed.

With their own materials ready to use, students could learn and participate in class with greater confidence.
