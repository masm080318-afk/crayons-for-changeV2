Project HINGYAP

Their own supplies. A place to begin.

57Students received learning kits
September 17, 2026Distribution date

THE SCHOOL

Guinciaman Elementary School

San Miguel, Leyte, Philippines

Learning kits for 57 students, selected with teachers’ guidance.

Teachers recommended recipients based on their need for school supplies. The school’s teachers and student government helped organize the distribution so the materials reached the selected learners.

Students received supplies for classroom activities, written exercises, and learning tasks at home.

Made possible through partnership

Crayons for Change funded the learning kits. Teach for the Philippines coordinated local distribution through its Teacher Fellows and school communities.
