Programs

Four practical programs: supplies, STEM camp, youth business education, and international outreach.

School Supply Drives

Crayons for Change collects school basics and turns them into supply kits students can use immediately in class.

ScheduleOngoing during the school year
Who it servesStudents missing basic classroom supplies
Current count696+ supplies delivered
  • Crayons, markers, colored pencils, and art supplies
  • Notebooks, folders, and loose-leaf paper
  • Backpacks and pencil cases
  • Rulers, scissors, glue sticks, and other classroom basics
  • Local distribution and international delivery, including Ahmedabad, India

Youth Business Workshops and Fair

Students learn how to plan, price, and present a small business idea, then bring it to a community fair. The program is education and fundraising at the same time: ticket revenue helps pay for student supplies.

Ticket$25 per student
Fair dateNovember 15
LocationChinmaya Mission Sacramento
  • Students practice picking a product, setting a price, explaining their idea, and speaking to visitors.
  • The public Zeffy page lists full-program access for the $25 registration.
  • Students can use the fair as a low-pressure first business experience.
  • Money raised helps fund crayons, notebooks, backpacks, and classroom tools for supply drives.

Free STEM Summer Camp

The STEM camp gives students free access to hands-on projects instead of just worksheets or videos.

ScheduleSummer sessions
CostFree for students
Current count116+ students supported

What campers learn

  • Basic coding and computational thinking
  • Engineering design through hands-on building challenges
  • Introduction to electronics and simple circuits
  • Problem-solving and teamwork
  • Creative STEM projects and science experiments
